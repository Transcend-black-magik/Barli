# Reference assets

Reference: https://dribbble.com/shots/27119940-BALI-HOTEL-Landing-page by Gapsy Studio.

`public/bali-sunset.jpg` is a photographic crop extracted from the original Dribbble presentation poster (1600 x 1200). It is the exact scene used in the supplied reference, rather than a stock substitute.

The heading, script, logo and paper-edge SVGs were traced from the presentation poster to reproduce the reference lettering and silhouette. Body copy and navigation are live HTML.

Source video: https://cdn.dribbble.com/userupload/46849820/file/0fbeaed0a8e89fc8f845b556ac83d31d.mp4
